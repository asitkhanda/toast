Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Toast (the menu bar / system tray application for macOS and Windows available at toast.asit.space) and its website handle information. Toast is developed independently and is not affiliated with Vercel Inc.

Summary

Toast is designed to keep your sensitive data on your device. The app stores your Vercel and/or Cloudflare API tokens and preferences locally. It communicates directly with Vercel’s and Cloudflare’s APIs to read deployment status. To improve reliability and fix bugs, Toast can optionally send anonymous usage data and crash reports to our analytics provider when “Help improve Toast” is enabled. We do not collect your name, email, API tokens, team or account, project names, or deployment details.

Information the app stores locally

On your device, Toast may store:

  • Your Vercel personal access token and/or Cloudflare API token, saved in the macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager
  • Your selected team, watched projects, and app preferences (for example, polling settings, notification preferences, and background behavior)
  • Cached deployment and project data needed to display status in the menu bar / system tray

This information stays on your device unless you remove it by disconnecting the app or uninstalling Toast.

Anonymous product analytics

When “Help improve Toast” is enabled (on by default; you can turn it off in Settings), Toast sends anonymous diagnostic and usage information to PostHog to help us understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. This data is used only to improve Toast — not for advertising or profiling.

What we may collect:

  • App version and build number
  • macOS or Windows version and device architecture (for example, arm64 or x64)
  • Anonymous feature usage (for example, onboarding completed, settings toggles)
  • API error types and HTTP status codes (not request contents)
  • Crash stack traces and anonymous diagnostic reports you choose to send
  • Optional feedback message text if you use Send Feedback

What we do not collect through analytics:

  • Your Vercel or Cloudflare tokens
  • Team or account IDs, project names, deployment URLs, or commit messages
  • Your name, email address, or other personal identifiers

PostHog assigns a random anonymous identifier on your device. We do not use analytics to spy on your deployment activity — only to understand app health and improve the product.

Information sent over the network

When you use Toast, the app may contact:

  • Vercel (vercel.com) — to validate your token and fetch teams, projects, and deployment status using read-only API requests
  • Cloudflare (cloudflare.com) — only if you connect Cloudflare, to validate your token and fetch accounts, Pages projects, Workers, and deployment status using read-only API requests
  • toast.asit.space — to check for app updates via the Sparkle appcast feed
  • PostHog (us.i.posthog.com) — anonymous usage and crash data when “Help improve Toast” is enabled

Toast does not send your Vercel or Cloudflare tokens to any server operated by the Toast developer. API requests go directly from your device to the provider.

Website

The Toast website provides a landing page, download redirect, and update feed. Like most websites, hosting infrastructure may log standard request metadata (such as IP address, browser type, and requested URL) for security and operations. The site does not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Toast uses local OS notifications to alert you when a watched deployment completes. Notification content is generated on your device from deployment data retrieved from Vercel or Cloudflare.

What we do not collect

  • No Toast account or login is required
  • No sale or sharing of personal information for advertising
  • No Vercel or Cloudflare tokens, project names, or deployment content sent to analytics

Third-party services

Your use of Vercel is governed by Vercel’s privacy policy and, if you use Cloudflare, by Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Analytics are processed by PostHog. Downloads are hosted on GitHub Releases; GitHub’s policies apply to that infrastructure.

Your choices

  • Turn off anonymous analytics and crash reporting in Toast Settings at any time
  • Disconnect your token at any time in Toast Settings
  • Remove the app and its Keychain / Credential Manager entry by uninstalling Toast
  • Use a read-only, team- or account-scoped Vercel or Cloudflare token with the minimum access you need

Children

Toast is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via GitHub Issues on the Toast repository.
